News on the Hill
Week 6 Edition  ·  Wednesday, June 17, 2026
"The names are familiar. The scores are tighter. And the stories keep getting better."

Five weeks are now in the books, and if we've learned anything, it's that the view from the top changes quickly around Pony Hills.

1st
Jim Hegg & Brad Selland
62.5 Points  ·  Team 12

The old boys are back on top.

Every season somebody wonders if Father Time has finally caught Jim and Brad. Every season they answer with another trip to the front page of the standings.

Five weeks in, the veterans have reclaimed first place and continue proving that experience still has a seat at the table.

No panic. Just progress.

2nd
Arin Boschee & Casey Beigh
60.5 Points  ·  Team 2

Nobody ever seems surprised to see Arin and Casey hanging around the top. That's because they always are.

The perennial contenders quietly remain within striking distance and seem perfectly content letting everyone else do the talking.

Two points back and lurking. Which is exactly where nobody wants them.

3rd
Mitch Korbel & Brandon Goergen
59.5 Points  ·  Team 9

Last week's leaders may have slipped to third, but don't mistake that for trouble.

Only three points separate first and third, and Team 9 remains very much in the hunt. After spending last season buried in the standings, Mitch and Brandon have become one of the biggest stories of the summer.

And while Brandon may still know exactly where Team 9 sits in the standings, league officials report his enthusiasm remains.

What's Next?

Can Jim and Brad hold off Father Time for another week?

Will Arin and Casey make their annual climb?

Can Mitch and Brandon reclaim the top spot?

And perhaps most importantly… Will anyone ever agree on what a circle means?

We'll find out soon enough.

🏡 Club House Chatter!!

A heated debate broke out during a recent pitch game involving a circled score.

One side argued the circle was simply being used to mark a team that had gone set. The opposing side immediately objected, noting that a circle on a scorecard has always meant one thing around Pony Hills: BIRDIE

The discussion quickly escalated into a history lesson involving one of the old-timers who reportedly had every handicap on the golf course calculated down to the decimal and knew exactly where every stroke belonged. Whether anyone actually remembers the math remains unclear.

What is clear is that the debate continued long after the hand was over. Then came the moment of the night. A few rounds later, the same team went set again. From somewhere around the table came the question:

"So does that mean two circles?"

League officials can confirm that laughter immediately followed and order was never fully restored.

This… is News on the Hill.
